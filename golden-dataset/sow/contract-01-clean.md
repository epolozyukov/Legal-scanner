# Statement of Work #SOW-2026-014

Between Acme Consulting LLC ("Vendor") and Northwind Retail Inc. ("Client"), effective March 1, 2026.

## 1. Services
Vendor will provide software implementation services as described in Exhibit A.

## 2. Fees and Payment
Client shall pay Vendor on a monthly basis in arrears, Net 30 from invoice date. Invoices unpaid after 30 days accrue interest at 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

## 3. Term and Termination
This SOW commences on the effective date and continues until completion of Services, unless terminated earlier. Either party may terminate this SOW for convenience upon 30 days' prior written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure such breach within 30 days after written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail. Upon any termination, Client shall pay Vendor for Services performed and non-cancellable expenses incurred through the effective date of termination.

## 4. Intellectual Property
All deliverables specifically created for Client under this SOW ("Work Product") shall be owned by Client upon full payment. Vendor retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing tools, methodologies, and software ("Background IP"), and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use any Background IP embedded in the Work Product solely as needed to use the Work Product.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party's Confidential Information shall remain confidential and shall not be disclosed to third parties, except as required by law. This obligation survives termination or expiration of this SOW for five (5) years, and indefinitely with respect to trade secrets.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Except for breaches of confidentiality or indemnification obligations, neither party's aggregate liability arising out of this SOW shall exceed the total fees paid or payable under this SOW in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 7. Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from (a) infringement of intellectual property rights, (b) breach of confidentiality obligations, or (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct of the indemnifying party.

## 8. Acceptance
Client shall review each deliverable against the acceptance criteria in Exhibit A within ten (10) business days of delivery. Deliverables are deemed accepted unless Client provides written notice of specific, material non-conformance within that window.

## 9. Change Orders
Any change to scope, timeline, or fees must be documented in a written change order signed by both parties before Vendor proceeds with the changed work.

## 10. Insurance
Vendor shall maintain, at its own expense: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, and (b) professional liability (errors & omissions) insurance of at least $1,000,000 per claim.

## 11. Governing Law
This SOW is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between the parties' executives, and if unresolved within 30 days, either party may pursue any remedy available in the state or federal courts located in Delaware.
