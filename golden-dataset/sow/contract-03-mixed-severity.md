# Statement of Work #SOW-2026-041

Between Brightline Analytics ("Vendor") and Client, effective June 1, 2026.

## 1. Services
Vendor will provide analytics dashboard development services as described in Exhibit A.

## 2. Fees and Payment
Client shall pay Vendor Net 45 from invoice date. Invoices shall be issued monthly.

## 3. Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this SOW for convenience upon 10 days' prior written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure such breach within 10 days after written notice.

## 4. Intellectual Property
Deliverables created under this SOW shall be owned by Client upon full payment.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party's confidential information shall remain confidential and not be disclosed to third parties. This obligation survives termination of this SOW for one (1) year.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Neither party's aggregate liability arising out of this SOW shall exceed a reasonable amount as determined in good faith by the parties.

## 7. Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from any and all third-party claims arising out of or related to this SOW.

## 8. Acceptance
Client shall review each deliverable within five (5) business days of delivery and confirm the deliverable is acceptable to Client's satisfaction.

## 9. Change Orders
Changes to scope shall be discussed between the parties' project leads and documented via email before proceeding.

## 10. Governing Law
This SOW is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario, Canada. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Ontario.
